iCOMMUNITY: Come on over for a cup of coffee and a morning pastry! Global Coffee Hour is meant to bring together both international and domestic students, scholars and families to celebrate our global community at UC San Diego. Join us for fun activities and build connections that expand across the world.

Food & Allergen Information

Global Coffee Hour offers free coffee, tea, and assorted pastries for attendees. Pastries typically include options that are vegetarian and nut-free, with a limited selection of vegan items.

  • Menu: brewed coffee, hot tea, and assorted breakfast pastries.
  • Dietary options: vegetarian and nut-free pastries; limited vegan options.
  • Common allergens: wheat/gluten, dairy, eggs; some items may contain soy. Nut-free items are prepared without nuts but may be produced in shared facilities.
